# Problem Statement

_Fill this in as a team before writing code. Keep it concrete and specific — this is the anchor for every decision that follows._

## The problem

_What specific Birmingham community problem are we addressing? Be concrete._

## Who is affected

_Who experiences this problem, and how directly?_

## Why it matters

_Why is this worth solving in a hack-a-thon timeframe? What's the evidence it's a real problem, not an assumed one?_

## Existing solutions

_What already exists that addresses this (even partially)? We are not trying to reinvent the wheel — list what's out there so we know what NOT to rebuild._

## Our approach

_What are we actually building, in one or two sentences? What makes it different or additive to what already exists?_

## Scope for this event

_What's realistically achievable in the time we have? What's explicitly out of scope?_

## Success looks like

_How will we know, by the end of the event, that we built something that works?_
